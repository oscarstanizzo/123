Who we are

A distributor built around the reorder, not the first order

Most of what our customers buy, they buy every week: gloves, liners, towel, cups, chemistry, labels. That is a logistics problem before it is a sales problem — so we built the company around inventory depth, four distribution centres and a cut-off late enough to matter.

The result is one purchase order instead of five, one invoice to reconcile, and one account manager who knows what you order without being told again.

How we work

Three commitments we will be held to

Stock depth over catalogue width

We would rather carry fewer SKUs and always have them than list 200,000 items and backorder a third of them.

Published prices, contract honoured

Volume tiers are on the product page. A contract price holds across every site on the account until we agree otherwise in writing.

A person, not a ticket queue

Every account has a named specialist who answers the phone and can fix a shipment without escalating it.

Sustainability

The eco-preferable line is the easy choice, on purpose

Compostable packaging, recycled-content paper and certified chemistry are flagged everywhere they appear and earn double rewards points. Dispensing systems that cut consumption get the same treatment.

We would rather sell you a controlled dispenser that halves your towel usage than another case of folded towel.
