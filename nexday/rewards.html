NexDay Rewards

Points on every dollar you already spend, double on the eco-preferable line, redeemed straight against your next invoice. No tiers to chase, no expiry games.

How it works

Four steps, no fine print worth hiding

  1. Enrol with your account

    Every trade account is enrolled automatically. Nothing to sign, no annual fee.

  2. Earn on every order

    One point per dollar on stocked products, including freight-free orders.

  3. Double on eco products

    Anything flagged Eco earns two points per dollar — compostables, recycled content, certified chemistry.

  4. Redeem against an invoice

    100 points equals $1 credit, applied at checkout or against your Net-30 statement.

Questions

Rewards FAQ

Do points expire?

Points stay on the account as long as it is active. After 24 consecutive months with no order, the balance is cleared — we email at 18 and 21 months first.

Can I combine rewards with a special offer?

Yes. Points accrue on the price you actually pay, including clearance and weekly specials.

Who can redeem on a multi-site account?

Whoever you name as the account administrator. Sites can earn into a shared pool or hold separate balances — your call at setup.

What counts as eco-preferable?

Compostable and recycled-content packaging, certified chemistry, and dispensing systems that measurably cut consumption. Those products carry the Eco badge everywhere they appear.
