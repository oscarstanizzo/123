Shipping & returns FAQ

Which items are NexDay Guaranteed?

Anything showing the NexDay badge on its product card. Roughly three quarters of the catalogue qualifies; special-order and drop-ship items do not.

What if only part of my order is guaranteed?

The guaranteed lines ship the same day and the rest follows. You are never charged twice for freight on a split order.

How do returns work?

Unopened, resalable stock comes back within 30 days for full credit. We arrange the pickup; there is no restocking fee on stocked items.

Something arrived damaged.

Photograph it and tell us within 48 hours. Replacement ships the same day and the claim is ours to chase with the carrier, not yours.

Do you deliver to residential or job-site addresses?

Yes, though tailgate and appointment delivery on skid orders carry a quoted surcharge shown before you confirm.
