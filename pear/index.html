01  —  Craft

We took the long way
around a very small object.

A phone body is usually cast, because casting is fast. The Ora is milled from solid, because milling is honest. What is left after eleven hours is eight per cent of what we started with — and a shell with no seam, no filler, and no glue.

  1. 01

    The billet

    Grade 5 titanium, certified to aerospace lot traceability. Each bar is assayed before it earns a serial.

    Ti 6Al‑4V
  2. 02

    Eleven hours

    Five-axis milling in eleven passes, the last of them cutting two microns at a time. Nothing about this is efficient.

    2 µm final pass
  3. 03

    The wheel

    Chamfers are lapped by hand against a cotton wheel — forty minutes per body, by one of nine people who are allowed to.

    40 min · by hand
  4. 04

    The face

    A 2.1 mm sapphire crystal, grown over sixteen days and cut with a diamond wire. It will outlive the phone.

    9 Mohs

02  —  Finishes

Four surfaces.
One of them is yours.

Titanium takes colour badly and light beautifully, so we stopped painting it. Each finish is the metal itself — anodised, blasted, or left alone.

03  —  Detail

Display

A screen you can read at noon,
and sleep beside at midnight.

6.3 inches of LTPO, tuned to 2,600 nits in daylight and one nit at 3 a.m. Below 40 per cent it stops using PWM entirely, because flicker is a physical thing and some people feel it.

  • 2,600 nits peak
  • 1–120 Hz adaptive
  • DC dimming below 40%
Optics

Three lenses ground in the same
shop as our sapphire.

A 1-inch main sensor behind an eight-element glass stack — not plastic, which is why the barrel is deep and the plateau is proud. Colour is calibrated against a physical chart, one phone at a time.

  • 1" main sensor
  • f/1.6 eight-element
  • Per-unit colour calibration
Silicon

Quiet, cool, and none of
your business.

The neural engine runs on the device. Transcription, search, photo understanding — none of it leaves the titanium. There is no account to make, and nothing to opt out of.

  • 3 nm process
  • 48 GB unified memory
  • On-device inference
Service

Ten years, and a person
who signs their name.

Every Ora is serialised to a watchmaker in Neuchâtel who will service it for a decade. Battery, screen, and camera modules are held in stock for the full term. Send it back; it comes home refinished.

  • 10 yr parts held
  • Named technician
  • Refinish included
